06 / About

Built from curiosity.
Run with intention.

I'm Naman Duggavathi. I got into this because I wanted to challenge myself by combining my passion for drones, photography, and editing with one of the largest industries in the world - all at the age of 13.

Everything I've created, including this website, is built from the ground up. Over the past several months, I've spent countless hours learning concepts and skills that have challenged me every step of the way, even when I got discouraged or progress was slow.

My goal isn't just to photograph properties, it's to consistently put my best effort into delivering the highest-quality imagery I can. Whether you're selling a family home or a luxury property, I'm committed to reliable, professional service and imagery that makes listings stand out.

Thanks for being here. If you're ever interested in aerial photography, contact me anytime.
